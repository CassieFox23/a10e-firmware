# disclaimer

this is one person's read of one firmware build, not a promise. the write-up and the scripts in `tools/` describe what was found on a Samsung Galaxy A10e (SM-A102U) with the stock build named in the [teardown](A10e-teardown.md). another build, carrier or model can differ.

## flashing is at your own risk

on this phone the bootloader refuses to flash while Knox Guard says it's locked, and the teardown says why. trying anyway, or flashing a phone that does let you, can leave it unable to boot. check every step against your own device first.
